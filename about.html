Masha Maria

Masha is a fashion and creative model based in Salt Lake City, with experience across editorial, commercial, lifestyle, film, television, and outdoor campaigns. Her work moves between refined minimalism and emotionally driven, character-focused imagery, bringing depth, presence, and authenticity to each project.

With a background in self-development, healing modalities, and emotionally immersive creative work, Masha approaches modeling through an intuitive and expressive lens. Her understanding of emotion, embodiment, and human connection allows her to bring nuance, rawness, and spontaneity to the camera in a way that feels grounded, cinematic, and emotionally resonant.

In addition to modeling, she has experience in independent film as both a performer and creative director, contributing to projects through visual storytelling, mood, and artistic collaboration. Known for her versatility and collaborative presence, Masha works closely with photographers, directors, brands, and creative teams to create imagery that feels alive and intentional.
